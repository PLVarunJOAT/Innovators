What it is // in plain words

People don't just “Google it” anymore. They ask.

For years, getting found online meant one thing: SEO, short for search engine optimization. That simply means helping Google show your business when someone searches for what you do.

Today people also ask AI tools like ChatGPT and Gemini, “Who is the best plumber near me?” Those tools answer with a short list of businesses. If you are not on that list, you are invisible to that customer.

GAS-EO is our method that covers all of it in one plan. Here is what the name stands for:

AAnswer boxesThe quick answers at the top of Google and from voice assistants. We help yours be the answer. (Also called AEO.)

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Straight answers in plain English.

What is GAS-EO?+

GAS-EO is Nov8rs' own method for getting your business found. It combines Generative AI optimization (being recommended by tools like ChatGPT), Answer engine optimization (being the quick answer on Google) and Search Engine Optimization (ranking in Google results and Maps) into one plan.

What is SEO, in simple terms?+

SEO stands for search engine optimization. It means improving your website and your Google listing so that Google shows your business higher when people search for what you do.

What are AEO and GEO?+

AEO means answer engine optimization: helping Google and voice assistants use your website as the answer to a question. GEO means generative engine optimization: helping AI tools like ChatGPT and Gemini mention and recommend your business.

Why is Nov8rs the only company offering GAS-EO?+

Because we created it. GAS-EO is our own name and our own method for running SEO, AEO and GEO as a single plan. Other SEO agencies may offer parts of it, but GAS-EO itself is only available from Nov8rs (Innovators).

How long does SEO take to work?+

SEO is not instant. Small improvements can show within weeks, and stronger results usually build over three to six months or more. It depends on your starting point and how much competition you have.

Can you get my business on the Google map?+

We work on everything that helps you appear in the map results: your Google Business Profile, reviews, local pages and listings. No honest agency can guarantee a specific spot, but these steps give you the best chance.

Do you offer SEO services across the USA?+

Yes. We are an SEO agency serving businesses across the United States. Everything is done remotely, and we focus on the towns and cities where your customers are.

Do I need a new website for GAS-EO?+

Not always. If your current website is in good shape, we improve it. If it is holding you back, we will tell you honestly and show you the options.
