What it is // in plain words

A CRM is your customer list, organized. Ours also answers for you.

CRM stands for customer relationship management. In plain words, it is one place that keeps every customer, every message and every job, so nothing gets forgotten.

Ours has AI built in. If you miss a call because you are on a job, it texts the caller right away, answers common questions and offers a time to book. That feature is called missed call text back.

It also sends reminders, follows up on quotes and asks for a review when the job is done. This is often called marketing automation: the repetitive follow-up happens by itself.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Straight answers in plain English.

What is a CRM?+

CRM stands for customer relationship management. It is one place to keep all your customers, messages and jobs, so you can see who contacted you, what was said and what happens next.

What is missed call text back?+

When you can't answer the phone, the system instantly sends the caller a text message, such as “Sorry we missed you, how can we help?” The conversation continues by text, so the customer doesn't call a competitor.

Can AI really answer my customers?+

Yes, for common questions such as opening hours, service areas and booking a time. You approve what it says, and anything it can't handle is passed to you or your team.

Can the AI make phone calls?+

Yes. AI voice agents can call new leads, follow up on quotes and confirm appointments, using a script you approve. Every call is logged so you can see what was said.

How do automated review requests work?+

After a job is marked complete, the customer automatically gets a text or email with a direct link to leave a Google review. More happy customers leave reviews because it is so easy.

Is it hard to use?+

No. If you can send a text message, you can use it. We set everything up and train you and your team.

Does it replace my staff?+

No. It handles the first replies and the repetitive follow-up, so your team can focus on customers and the actual work.

Does it work with my website and ads?+

Yes. Website forms, ad leads, calls and social messages all arrive in the same inbox, and we connect it to the tools you already use.
