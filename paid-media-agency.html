What it is // in plain words

Ads put you at the top, today.

When someone searches “roof repair near me”, the first results they see are often ads. Paid media means paying to be in those top spots on Google, Bing, Facebook, Instagram or LinkedIn.

You choose a daily budget, so you never spend more than you planned. We write the ads, choose who sees them, and track every call and form back to the ad that brought it.

Some people call this PPC (pay-per-click) or performance marketing. It all means the same thing: you pay for ads, and we make sure that money turns into customers.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Straight answers in plain English.

What does a paid media agency do?+

A paid media agency plans, writes and manages your online ads. Nov8rs (Innovators) runs ads on Google, Microsoft (Bing), Facebook, Instagram and LinkedIn, tracks the calls they bring, and keeps improving them.

How much should I spend on ads?+

It depends on your industry and area. We suggest a starting budget on our first call. The ad spend itself is paid directly by you to Google or Meta, so you always stay in control.

How fast will I get calls?+

Ads can start bringing visitors soon after they go live. Results usually improve over the first few weeks as we learn which ads and searches work best.

Which is better, Google Ads or Facebook ads?+

Google Ads reach people who are searching for your service right now. Facebook and Instagram ads reach people who may need you soon. We recommend the right mix for your business.

What is the difference between ads and SEO?+

Ads put you at the top right away, but you pay for each click. SEO (our GAS-EO service) takes longer, but the calls keep coming without paying per click. Many businesses use both.

Do you run Microsoft Advertising and Bing ads?+

Yes. We manage Microsoft Advertising, which shows your ads on Bing. It often costs less per click than Google and reaches customers many businesses miss.

Do you offer LinkedIn ads?+

Yes. As a LinkedIn ads agency for business-to-business companies, we target the industries and job titles you want to reach.

Can you fix the ads I'm already running?+

Yes. We review your account, fix the tracking, cut the wasted spend and rebuild what isn't working.
