FAQ

Questions, answered.

Plain answers about Nov8rs (Innovators) and how we can help your business.

What does Nov8rs do?+

Nov8rs (you say it “innovators”) is a digital marketing agency in the USA. We help businesses get more customers online. We build websites, get you found on Google and AI tools, run your online ads, and set up an AI assistant that answers your calls and messages.

What is GAS-EO?+

GAS-EO is our own method for getting your business found. It covers three things at once: Generative AI tools like ChatGPT, Answer boxes on Google, and classic Search Engine Optimization (SEO). In short, wherever people search or ask, your business shows up.

Do you work with small local businesses?+

Yes. Most of our clients are local and service businesses such as plumbers, electricians, roofers, dentists, law firms and contractors. If your customers find you by searching online or asking for a recommendation, we can help.

I'm not a tech person. Is that a problem?+

Not at all. We explain everything in plain English, set everything up for you, and send simple reports that show calls and customers, not confusing charts.

How much does it cost?+

It depends on which services you need. Our instant quote builder lets you pick what you want and shows your price in about two minutes, with no sales call.

How soon will I see results?+

Online ads can start bringing calls soon after they launch. Getting found on Google takes longer and usually builds over a few months. We tell you what to expect before you start.

Do I need all four services?+

No. Many clients start with one, such as a new website or Google Ads, and add more later. The quote builder lets you choose only what you need.

How do I get started?+

Open the instant quote builder, pick your services and see your price. If you would rather talk to a person first, send us a message and we will reply within one business day.
